How Much Teacher Remediation Does Competency-Based Peer Apprenticeship Need? Evidence from 44 Education Systems in TIMSS 2023

Krisztina Varlaki · Manuscript in preparation

In a competency-based school, teachers do not have to remediate every learner who struggles. Most can be supported by a near-peer mentor, as long as one is available in their own school. Using TIMSS 2023 data from 297,262 students in 44 education systems, this study estimates how many learners lack such a mentor and would therefore need a teacher.

1.7–13.2%of learners need a teacher in mathematics, depending on the system
1.9–11.5%of learners need a teacher in science, depending on the system

That makes teacher remediation a small group that can be identified in advance, rather than the default.

The full paper will be posted here after peer review. This study tests a central assumption of the Distributed Mastery Architecture.
